How it works

Up and running in 3 quick steps

01

Sign in with Google

One click. We request only the drive.appdata + drive.file scopes — a private app folder JobPilot can't see anything else in. Your resume gets saved there, not on our server.

02

Paste your API keys in Settings

Open ⚙ Settings and paste your Anthropic key (required for AI) plus any of RapidAPI / Adzuna / USAJobs (optional, free tiers available). Keys are encrypted with AES-GCM in your browser — never sent to our server.

03

Search, tailor, export

Search 6 job boards in one shot. Tailor your resume to any job in seconds with Claude. Score for ATS, export as PDF or DOCX. Every API call goes browser → provider directly — your data never touches our server.

BYOK setup

The keys you'll paste in Settings

JobPilot never proxies your API calls. Bring your own provider keys — one required, the rest optional — and the browser talks to each provider directly. All keys live encrypted in your browser's localStorage and (optionally) sync via your Google Drive appDataFolder. Skip any optional provider; the corresponding job board just won't appear in results.
